2. **Clone** your fork locally
3. **Create a branch:** `git checkout -b add-meatloaf`
4. **Edit** the README.md — add meatloaf in the right section (alphabetical order usually)
5. **Commit:** `git commit -m "Add meatloaf to [section name]"`
